# 🧠 01. Core Readiness & Analytical Fundamentals

This folder contains resources, exercises, and frameworks dedicated to building analytical acumen, structured problem-solving skills, and interview readiness for Data Analyst and Business Intelligence roles.

---

## 🎯 Purpose & Scope

Before diving into complex code or database queries, strong core competencies in business reasoning, data interpretation, and quantitative logic are essential. This module serves as the foundation for:

* **Quantitative Aptitude**: Business math, profit & loss, margin calculations, percentage changes, ratios, weighted averages, and compound growth rates (CAGR).
* **Logical & Analytical Reasoning**: Pattern recognition, deductive logic, and structured problem breakdown.
* **Business Acumen & Frameworks**:
  * Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys, Fishbone / Ishikawa diagram).
  * Issue Tree & MECE (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) problem solving.
  * KPI definition and metrics decomposition (North Star metrics, operational vs. strategic KPIs).
* **Interview Readiness**: Technical question banks, resume project walk-through notes, portfolio review checklists, and behavioral scenarios (STAR method).

## 📋 Recommended Roadmap

1. **Aptitude Mastery**: Review basic business mathematics and arithmetic shortcuts.
2. **Business Case Studies**: Practice translating open-ended business problems into measurable analytical questions.
3. **Communication & Storytelling**: Practice summarizing technical insights into clear executive summaries and recommendations.
